The thesis

A tool for deeper practice — not a shortcut around it.

AI should make the work heavier, not lighter. A good prompt is a question you hadn't thought to ask. It slows you down, sharpens your attention, and sends you back to scripture with something to look for.

Built for men. Open to all.

Disciple AI is written for men who take faith seriously and want to grow with care. The frame is steady; the door is wide. If the questions are good, they're good for everyone at the table.

A week in the practice

One prompt lands every Wednesday morning. Ten minutes if you want them. A whole week if you have it.

i.
Wed 06:00 — the prompt lands

One passage, one question, one skill to practice.

Straight to your inbox. No notifications. No streaks. Friction is the feature.

ii.
Your own time — read first

Scripture comes before the prompt.

Read the passage twice. Then open the prompt. Then sit with what you found.

iii.
When you're ready — work it

Push back. Bring it to the group.

Take the question into prayer, into conversation, into Sunday. The AI is a second mind, not the last word.
