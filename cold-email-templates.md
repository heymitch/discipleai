# Disciple AI — Cold Email Templates

Four camp-calibrated templates. Each is ~140–180 words. The `[PERSONALIZED_OPENER]` slot is filled from the `personalized_opener` column in `church-ai-leaders-contacts.csv` — one sentence per contact that proves you know their specific AI work.

The core framing is constant across all four versions: **honest broker, not pastor, hosting all four camps, no single Christian prescription.** Only the value prop and ask shift per camp.

---

## 1. Doomer / "Resist" template

**Subject:** Hosting your AI take in Disciple AI — even if it's faithful disengagement

Hi [Name],

[PERSONALIZED_OPENER]

I run Disciple AI, a newsletter for church leaders trying to think clearly about AI. I'm not a pastor — I'm trying to lead smart through this moment because no one really has it figured out. My editorial posture is honest broker: I host all four camps in the current AI-and-church discourse, and the eschatological-discernment voice your work represents is essential to that, not a foil to it.

Best case for me would be to feature your resources for readers who want to handle AI faithfully — even (especially) if your counsel is principled disengagement. I'd treat that as one valid Christian response, not as something to argue with.

Would you be open to either (a) letting me feature an excerpt or link to your existing AI work, or (b) a short Q&A I could run as a guest piece?

Either way, thanks for the work — it's shaping how a lot of people think about this.

[Signature]

## Notes on use

- **Personalization line is non-negotiable.** Every opener in the CSV references a *specific* piece of work — a book title, a specific essay, a specific episode. If you can't find one, the right move is to not send the email, not to fall back on generic flattery.
- **Subject-line variants worth testing:** For the Doomer template specifically, the "even if it's faithful disengagement" phrase is the differentiator — most outreach from AI-friendly publications doesn't lead with that, so it earns the open. Don't water it down.
- **The three-option ask** at the bottom is intentional: it lets a busy person reply with the lowest-friction option instead of saying yes/no to one big ask. Reply rates go up materially.
- **What to skip:** No "I hope this email finds you well." No "I'm a huge fan." No promises about audience size. The framing is the value prop; don't dilute it.
