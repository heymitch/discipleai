# Interview notes — [first name initial]

## Filename convention

Save copies of this template as `YYYY-MM-DD-firstname-initial-archetype.md` in this directory (`interviews/notes/`).

Example: `2026-06-12-j-builder.md`

## Context

Two lines max. Who they are, what they lead or do. Specific enough to be human, generic enough to protect them at the attribution level they chose.

## Their result

Quiz archetype: [archetype]. Where they sit relative to that result. Does it fit them cleanly, are they at an edge between two archetypes, did the result surprise them. Reference the dimension scores if any one of them is doing unusual work for the overall archetype.

## The moment

The one sentence or admission that surfaced the thing they hadn't expected to find. Verbatim where possible. This is the seed that could become a quote in Email 4, the book, or the transcripts library.

## Surrounding context for the moment

What was happening in the conversation right before the moment. What shifted after it. The context matters as much as the quote.

## The micro-shift, if any

What the moment did. Not what it "meant." What did the person say or notice or decide differently in the rest of the conversation. Resist interpretation. Record observation.

## Quotes worth keeping

Each quote gets context and a publish-readiness flag. Flag = `green` (clear to use at chosen attribution), `yellow` (needs explicit re-permission), `red` (do not publish).

- `green` "Quote 1" — context for what the quote responds to
- `yellow` "Quote 2" — context, and the reason it needs re-confirmation
- `red` "Quote 3" — context, and why it stays internal

## Disagreements I had with them

The honest version. Where my read of the question and theirs diverged. What I pushed on, what they pushed back on, what didn't get resolved.

This section feeds the future transcripts library, which is meant to show prospective interviewees that disagreement is allowed and doesn't ruin the conversation. If this section is empty, the call was either pure agreement (rare) or I held back (likely). Mark which.

## What I learned

One or two sentences. Not a summary of the whole call. The thing I didn't know before this conversation that I know now.

## What I would have asked if we had another 30 minutes

The unfinished thread. The question that opened up at minute 27 of a 30-minute call.
