## Section 1 — Your result

The quiz pulls people toward one of five archetypes. Each one is a real position, and a coherent one, with its own theology and practice behind it.

- **The Watchman** reads the AI moment as a sign of the times and keeps the people he leads anchored in truth that doesn't move.
- **The Steward** treats AI as a powerful magnifier of who we already are, and invests in formation that can hold complexity.
- **The Builder** treats AI as a tool, and asks where it serves the mission and where it doesn't, with clear human ownership at every step.
- **The Pioneer** sees AI as a creative gift that could serve the church's calling, and is imagining what's now possible in mission, translation, equipping.
- **The Preservationist** holds that embodied, communal, sacramental life is precisely what AI cannot touch, and protects the practices that make that life.

Wherever you landed, these are the first questions:

1. What did your result get right?
2. What did it get wrong, or miss?
3. How long have you been near this stance, and what put you here?

## Section 2 — Questions specifically for your archetype

Pick the section that matches your quiz result. If you landed between two archetypes (which happens), pick the one you'd defend more easily and we'll get to the other one later.

### If you're a Watchman

1. What signs are you reading most closely right now?
2. When you say a person needs to be anchored in truth that doesn't move, what does that look like for someone in your church on a Tuesday afternoon, not on a Sunday morning?
3. Where are you afraid of being wrong?
4. What's the temptation specific to your archetype that you've felt in yourself?

### If you're a Steward

1. What's a guideline or practice you've actually put in place around AI, or one you wish you had?
2. What's the formation gap you're most worried about in the people you lead?
3. Who is doing this work well that you're learning from?
4. What's the temptation specific to your archetype that you've felt in yourself?

### If you're a Builder

1. What's one AI use case where the practice in your context is now genuinely good?
2. Where is your line, and why is it there and not five steps earlier or five steps later?
3. What's the workflow that surprised you with how much it kept human ownership intact?
4. What's the temptation specific to your archetype that you've felt in yourself?

### If you're a Pioneer

1. What's a kingdom-work use of AI you've actually seen done well, not just imagined?
2. In ten years, what does your most hopeful vision for the church and AI look like? What does the most worried version look like?
3. Who do you wish was paying more attention to this?
4. What's the temptation specific to your archetype that you've felt in yourself?

### If you're a Preservationist

1. What's the practice in your church or home that's doing the real work, that AI couldn't touch even if it tried?
2. What's the conversation about AI you keep getting pulled into that you wish people would stop having?
3. If you had to use AI for one thing because someone made you, what would it be?
4. What's the temptation specific to your archetype that you've felt in yourself?

## Section 3 — Questions for everyone

1. What's the conversation about AI you wish your church or community was having, and isn't?
2. Where do you feel most certain about your stance? Where do you feel least?
3. What would change your mind?
4. Who in your life is asking different questions about AI than you are? What do they see that you don't?
5. If you could put one sentence about AI on the wall of every church, what would it say?

## Section 4 — Practice, not theology

1. What does engaging with AI actually look like in your week? Where does it show up?
2. Where have you decided not to use it, and what shaped that decision?
3. What practice in your life would you protect from AI even if AI got ten times better than it is today?

## Section 5 — The one that usually matters most

There's one question that tends to surface what people came to talk about, even when they thought they came to talk about something else.

What's the thing about AI you've been wanting to say out loud, and haven't yet found the right room to say it in?

## What happens with your answers

Your responses shape the book and the tool that come out of this project. You decide the attribution. Three options:

- **Full attribution.** Your name and role on the record.
- **First name and rough context.** Identifying details softened.
- **Fully anonymous.** No traceable identifiers. Archetype only if relevant.

If you change your mind later, you can update your preference. Nothing gets published with stronger attribution than the level you're comfortable with the day before publication. If you ever want a specific quote pulled, that's fine too, no questions asked.
