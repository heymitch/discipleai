# Interviews

This directory holds the artifacts that support the 1:1 conversations behind the Disciple AI book and tool. Conversations originate from the quiz-to-interview nurture sequence (see [../emails/](../emails/)).

## What's in here

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [questionnaire.md](questionnaire.md) | The flexible interview structure. Sent to anyone who books sync or replies "async" — works as both an async questionnaire and a sync pre-call preview. |
| [notes/TEMPLATE.md](notes/TEMPLATE.md) | The capture template for each completed interview. Feeds the real-story rewrite of [Email 4](../emails/email-4-day-12-HOLD.md). |
| [transcripts/README.md](transcripts/README.md) | Spec for the future sample-transcript section that gets added to the questionnaire once real interviews accumulate. |

## How the artifacts work together

1. Someone completes the quiz and enters the nurture sequence.
2. They book on Calendly or reply "async."
3. They receive [questionnaire.md](questionnaire.md) as the reply, with the appropriate intro section selected based on their format choice.
4. Interview happens (sync) or answers come back (async).
5. Interviewer fills out a copy of [notes/TEMPLATE.md](notes/TEMPLATE.md) for that conversation.
6. Confirmed-permission moments from those notes flow back into Email 4 (replacing the placeholder story) and into [transcripts/](transcripts/) once enough exist.

## Tone principles

- **Every archetype is the strong form of a real position.** No one's quiz result is the wrong answer. The questionnaire and notes language treat all five stances as legitimate.
- **The interviewer is exploring, not adjudicating.** Questions are designed to help the interviewee find what they haven't put words to, not to test their orthodoxy.
- **Disagreement is allowed and named.** The notes template includes a "disagreements I had with them" section precisely because the future transcripts library needs honest disagreement examples to do its anxiety-reducing job.
- **Attribution is the interviewee's call, and reversible.** They can downgrade attribution any time before publication. Nothing gets used at stronger attribution than they're comfortable with the day before publication.

## Five archetypes the quiz measures

Source: [../ai-quiz/content/questions.json](../../ai-quiz/content/questions.json)

| Archetype | Strong-form summary |
|---|---|
| **Watchman** | Reads the AI moment as a spiritual sign. Keeps people anchored in truth that doesn't move. Discerns the signs of the times. |
| **Steward** | Treats AI as a powerful magnifier of who we already are. Invests in formation that can hold complexity. |
| **Builder** | Treats AI as a tool. Asks where it serves the mission and where it doesn't, with clear human ownership at every step. |
| **Pioneer** | Sees AI as a creative gift that could serve the church's calling. Imagines what's now possible in mission, translation, equipping. |
| **Preservationist** | Holds that embodied, communal, sacramental life is precisely what AI cannot touch. Protects the practices that make that life. |

Dimensions measured across the 11 questions: theological clarity, pastoral integrity, operational alignment, cultural discernment, discipleship formation, worldview drift.
