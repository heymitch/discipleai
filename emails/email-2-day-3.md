# Email 2 — Day 3 — Five Archetypes Keep Showing Up

## Subject line options

- A: Five postures keep showing up
- B: The pattern in your answers
- C: What the quiz is actually measuring

## Preview text

Each one is the strong form of a real position.

## Body

The quiz pulls people toward one of five archetypes, and almost everyone lands clearly in one of them.

The Watchman reads the AI moment as a sign of the times, and keeps the people he leads anchored in truth that doesn't move.

The Steward treats AI as a powerful magnifier of who we already are, and invests in formation that can hold complexity.

The Builder treats AI as a tool, and asks where it serves the mission and where it doesn't, with clear human ownership at every step.

The Pioneer sees AI as a creative gift that could serve the church's calling, and is imagining what's now possible in mission, translation, and equipping.

The Preservationist holds that embodied, communal, sacramental life is precisely what AI cannot touch, and protects the practices that make that life.

Each of those is the strong form of a real position. None of them is the wrong answer.

The most interesting part of what's coming back to me from the early conversations is this. The five postures don't actually disagree about whether the AI question matters. They disagree about which part of the question to answer first, and which part they'd be unwilling to give up to answer it.

That's the disagreement worth having out loud.

Wherever you landed, your result is the start of a conversation, not the end of one.

If the quiz landed somewhere real for you, send it to one other person at your church and see which archetype the quiz returns for them. The interesting moment is when two people who land in different archetypes try to explain to each other what they're actually protecting.

Mitch
